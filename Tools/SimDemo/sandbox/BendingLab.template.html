    Physics parameters

    How it works. Every bent thing is a volume with mass (kg), temperature (K) and velocity. Each 1/60 s step, overlapping volumes exchange heat (Q = h·A·ΔT·dt, with ice→water→steam phase changes), momentum (drag ½ρCdA|v|v, inelastic coupling) and mass (steam boiling off, air entrained into flame, water soaking into soil), and nothing is created or destroyed. Steam rises by buoyancy and condenses; wet ground loses traction until it dries.
