Bending Training Ground

Walk the field as a bender: four elements, five moves each. Everything you bend is simulated in real units: the whip is 20 kg of pond water (only near a pond), water puts out fire, a wall is tonnes of soil moved from the trench beside it, and wind feeds flames and knocks dummies over.

WaterWhip from a pond, lash, freeze, splash mud, ice daggers.
EarthHurl rocks, raise walls, sculpt terrain, earthquake.
FireBlasts, flame stream, jet dash, ring of fire.
AirBlasts, gusts, air scooter, air jump, tornado.
