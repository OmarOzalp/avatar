Bending Training Ground

Walk the field as a bender and try all four elements. Everything you touch is simulated in real units: the whip is 20 kg of pond water, a wall is tonnes of soil moved from the trench beside it, fire is a hot gas that boils water and melts ice, and wind feeds flames and knocks dummies over.
